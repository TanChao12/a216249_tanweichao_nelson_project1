Project 1: GapFinder
Name: Tan Wei Chao
Matric: A216249

Problem Statement
Many low-income communities in Malaysia remain in poverty because essential economic opportunities and services are not identified or mapped at a local level. Without data-driven insights, it is difficult for NGOs and local entrepreneurs to know where to intervene.

Impact
This lack of information leads to inefficient resource allocation and economic stagnation. Communities remain underserved because the 'gaps' in basic services—like affordable retail or healthcare—are invisible to those who can provide them.

Solution
GapFinder is a mobile application designed to log and analyze regional economic needs. It empowers stakeholders to identify 'Opportunity Gaps,' driving targeted impact and promoting financial inclusion to help alleviate poverty.
